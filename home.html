Welcome

Eternal Versace is a multimedia online exhibition that tells the story of the House of Versace: its origins, its creative vision, and its lasting influence on fashion and popular culture. Through garments, accessories, photographs, publications, and design objects, the exhibition offers a closer look at the ideas and imagination behind one of the world’s most recognizable fashion houses.

Visitors are invited to explore the evolution of Versace, from Gianni Versace’s early work to the later creations shaped by Donatella Versace. Along the way, the exhibition highlights the elements that made the house so distinctive: references to classical mythology, rich ornament, sensual silhouettes, and a love of drama and glamour. It also shows Versace’s strong connection to music, celebrity culture, and contemporary design.

But Eternal Versace is about more than fashion alone. It is also a story of creativity, transformation, and the power of a strong visual identity. Versace changed the meaning of luxury by bringing together art, performance, sensuality, and innovation in a way that felt bold and unforgettable.

By combining fashion history with digital storytelling, Eternal Versace discovers how Versace became not only a fashion house, but a true cultural symbol.
