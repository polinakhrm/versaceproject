Not just clothing

Beyond clothing, Versace has produced a wide range of luxury products since the 1980s. These include fragrances (launched in 1981), accessories such as handbags, sunglasses, and jewelry, as well as watches and fine leather goods. The house also expanded into home décor with Versace Home in the 1990s, offering furniture, porcelain, and textiles. In addition, Versace has developed hotels and lifestyle spaces, most notably through the Palazzo Versace properties, further establishing itself as a global luxury lifestyle brand.
